- Heavy rainfall has been affecting Thailand since early November, causing flash floods and river overflow that have resulted in casualties and damage. The most affected provinces are Ayutthaya, Nakhon Sawan, Suphan Buri, Phichit, Sukhothai, Phitsanulok, Lop Buri, Nakhon Pathom, Chai Nat, Ang Thong, Sing Buri, Uthai Thani, Kamphaeng Phet, Uttaradit, Nonthaburi, Phetchabun, and Pathum Thani in northern, central, and northeastern Thailand.
- According to the International Federation of Red Cross and Red Crescent Societies (IFRC), as of 12 November, at least 16 people died, 467,600 people affected, and 135,762 houses affected among 2,882 villages.
- On 13-14 November, local thunderstorms are expected over the western part of the country.
